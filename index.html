Something From My Heart

A little letter for the girl who makes my days more special.

To my baby, Wulan ♡ Happy birthday to my favorite girl. ♡ I wish I could put every little thing I feel for you into words, but somehow, even a whole page doesn't feel like enough. Thank you for being you. Thank you for the little moments, the laughs, the silly things, and the memories that mean more to me than you might realize. You don't have to be perfect to be special to me. You can be cute, annoying, funny, dramatic, or all of them in one day — and somehow, you're still my favorite person. 😭 I hope this new chapter brings you more reasons to smile, more dreams to chase, and more moments that make you proud of yourself. When things get difficult, I hope you remember that you deserve kindness, patience, and happiness, always. And selfishly, I hope I get to be part of many more of your little moments. More pictures, more laughs, more stories that we'll look back on someday. Happy birthday, my love. I'm so glad our story began. With all my love, Your future husband (manifesting 😭💍)
